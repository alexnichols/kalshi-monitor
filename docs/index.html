About the data

A GitHub Actions job runs kalshi_pipeline.py every night and commits data.json next to this page, which loads it automatically. Until that file exists, or where it can’t be reached, the page falls back to the built-in snapshot. Load data file still works for a one-off file.

  • Pipeline file (primary): every Kalshi fill from Kalshi’s daily trade report (the file behind kalshi.com/market-data; one row per fill, identical to the API trade tape), by Kalshi’s reporting day in Eastern time, categorized by Kalshi’s series metadata, and priced with Kalshi’s fee schedule using each series’ and event’s fee terms in force on the trade date. Kalshi perps from every fill on the public perps trade tape, priced with Kalshi’s published perps fee tiers; Hyperliquid daily notional as exact bounds from its public candles.
  • Categories: Kalshi’s own series category. Sports, Crypto, Commodities, Elections, Politics, Financials, Economics and Climate & weather keep their names; combos (KXMVE tickers) are Combos; Kalshi’s other categories (Entertainment, Mentions, Science & technology, Companies, World, Health and smaller ones) are grouped as Other. Series missing from Kalshi’s series list are looked up through the market and event endpoints.
  • Fee terms by date: Kalshi’s change log (/series/fee_changes, from Oct 4, 2025) and current series terms; before the log, the published fee schedules in force at the time (archived copies of Jan, Feb, May, Jun, Jul and Sep 2025), including S&P 500 / Nasdaq-100 markets at 0.035 until Jul 3, 2026 and flat maker fees of 0.0025 per contract from May 13 to Jul 7, 2025.
  • What the public tape can’t show: Kalshi rounds fees per order, and whether an account rounds to the centicent (direct) or the cent (brokered); the report shows fills, without the taker side or (since mid-2026) the time of day, and the Volume tab’s premium measure is contracts × Yes price for the same reason. Fees are therefore given as a per-fill figure with an exact rounding range at list price. That range is not a bound on what Kalshi collects: customers trading through a broker pay Kalshi a flat $0.01 per contract per side instead (Robinhood Derivatives’ fee schedule), market makers with a Kalshi agreement may pay reduced fees (Kalshi’s Market Maker Program), and Kalshi runs fee-waiver programs. None of these is marked in the public report, so they are not applied. Rewards, rebates and broker payouts are paid separately and are not deducted either. Perps fees depend on each account’s 30-day volume tier, so they are shown as a tier range unless you choose tiers; no perps fee schedule is in hand for May 29 to Jul 6, 2026, so those days carry no fees.
  • Built-in snapshot (until the pipeline runs): Kalshi category volume from TickerTracker, which compiles Kalshi’s published daily market data; venue and perps volumes from DeFi Rate. The snapshot has no fees: fees appear only once trade-level data is loaded.
  • Polymarket volumes still come from DeFi Rate’s venue data; the pipeline does not pull Polymarket yet.
  • Partial periods: category volumes for November 2026 cover Nov 1 to 12; venue and perps figures run through Nov 13.
